## Getting Started

### Installing the App

1. Get the app file (APK) from your supervisor
2. Open the file on your Android phone
3. If prompted, allow installation from unknown sources
4. Wait for the installation to complete
5. Open the app from your home screen

### First Time Setup

When you first open the app, you'll see the login screen. Your supervisor will provide you with:
- Your phone number (username)
- Your PIN code

---

## Logging In

### How to Log In

1. Open the Rugyeyo app
2. Enter your phone number
3. Enter your 4-digit PIN
4. Tap **Login**

### Forgot Your PIN?

1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot PIN?**
2. Enter your phone number
3. Answer your security questions
4. Create a new PIN
5. Confirm your new PIN

### Security Questions

If this is your first time logging in, you may be asked to set up security questions. These help you recover your account if you forget your PIN.

1. Choose 3 questions from the list
2. Type your answers (remember these!)
3. Tap **Save**

After logging in, you'll see the main dashboard with these options:

| Button | What It Does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Harvest Recording** | Record coffee harvests from blocks |
| **My Tasks** | View and complete assigned tasks |
| **Farmer Aggregation** | Record coffee collected from farmers |
| **Processing** | Record processing activities |

The dashboard also shows:
- Your name and role
- Number of records waiting to sync
- Quick stats for today

## Recording Harvests

Use this to record coffee harvested from farm blocks.

### Step-by-Step: Record a Harvest

1. From the dashboard, tap **Harvest Recording**
2. Select the **Block** (use dropdown or scan QR code)
3. Enter the **Weight** in kilograms
4. Select the **Cherry Type** (red, green, mixed)
5. Select the **Harvest Method** (selective, strip)
6. Add any **Notes** (optional)
7. Tap **Save Harvest**

### Scanning Block QR Codes

If blocks have QR codes:
1. Tap the **QR icon** next to the block field
2. Point your camera at the QR code
3. The block will be selected automatically

### Viewing Past Harvests

1. In the Harvest screen, scroll down
2. You'll see a list of today's harvests
3. Tap any record to see details

## Managing Tasks

Tasks are jobs assigned to you by supervisors through the web system.

### Viewing Your Tasks

1. From the dashboard, tap **My Tasks**
2. You'll see a calendar with your tasks
3. Tap any date to see tasks for that day
4. Tasks show their status with colored badges

### Task Status Colors

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Light Brown | Assigned (waiting for you) |
| Medium Brown | Accepted (ready to start) |
| Dark Brown | In Progress (you're working on it) |
| Very Dark Brown | Completed |

### Accepting a Task

1. Tap on a task to open it
2. Read the task details
3. Tap **Accept** to accept the task
4. Or tap **Reject** if you cannot do it

### Starting a Task

After accepting:
1. Open the task again
2. Tap **Start Task**
3. The timer begins tracking your work

### Completing a Task

1. Open your in-progress task
2. Take photos as proof of completion:
   - Tap **Camera** to take a new photo
   - Or tap **Gallery** to choose existing photos
3. Add a comment (optional)
4. Tap **Complete Task**

### Adding Your Own Tasks

1. In My Tasks, tap the **+** button
2. Fill in the task details:
   - Title (what you need to do)
   - Activity type
   - Date
   - Priority (low, medium, high)
3. Tap **Add Task**

## Farmer Aggregation

Use this to record coffee collected from individual farmers.

### Recording Farmer Coffee

1. From dashboard, tap **Farmer Aggregation**
2. Search for the farmer by name or ID
3. Or scan the farmer's QR code
4. Enter the weight of coffee
5. Select the quality grade
6. Confirm the price per kg
7. Review the total payment
8. Tap **Save Record**

### Creating Vouchers

After saving, you can create a voucher:
1. The voucher shows farmer details and amount
2. Tap **Print Voucher** if connected to a printer
3. Or tap **Share** to send via WhatsApp

## Processing Records

Record what happens to coffee after harvest.

### Recording Processing

1. From dashboard, tap **Processing**
2. Select the processing type:
   - Washing
   - Fermentation
   - Drying
   - Hulling
3. Enter the batch details
4. Record input and output weights
5. Add any quality notes
6. Tap **Save**

## Working Offline

The app works even without internet connection!

### What Happens Offline

- All your records are saved on your phone
- You'll see a "Not synced" indicator
- A number shows how many records need syncing
- Keep working - nothing is lost

### When You're Back Online

Your records will sync automatically, or you can:
1. Go to dashboard
2. Tap **Sync Data**
3. Wait for the upload to complete

---

## Syncing Your Data

Syncing uploads your records to the main system.

### How to Sync

1. Make sure you have internet connection
2. From the dashboard, tap **Sync Data**
3. Watch the progress indicator
4. Wait for "Sync Complete" message

### Sync Status

| Message | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| "All synced" | Everything is uploaded |
| "X records pending" | Some records need internet |
| "Sync failed" | Check your connection and try again |

### Automatic Sync

The app also syncs automatically when:
- You log in
- You open the dashboard (if connected)
- The app detects a new internet connection

## Common Questions

### Q: The app says "Network Error"
**A:** Check your internet connection. Try:
- Turn WiFi off and on
- Move to an area with better signal
- Wait a few minutes and try again

### Q: I can't log in
**A:** Make sure:
- Your phone number is correct
- Your PIN is correct (4 digits)
- You have internet connection
- If you forgot your PIN, use "Forgot PIN?"

### Q: My records aren't showing on the web
**A:** Your records might not be synced yet:
1. Check if you have pending records (number on dashboard)
2. Connect to internet
3. Tap "Sync Data"
4. Wait for completion

### Q: The camera isn't working
**A:** The app needs camera permission:
1. Go to your phone's Settings
2. Find the Rugyeyo app
3. Tap Permissions
4. Enable Camera

### Q: How do I know if a task is from my supervisor?
**A:** Tasks from supervisors show the assigned person's name and have status badges. Tasks you create yourself won't have assignment information.

### Q: Can I edit a completed task?
**A:** No, once a task is completed and synced, it cannot be changed. Contact your supervisor if there's an error.

### Q: The app is slow
**A:** Try these steps:
1. Close other apps on your phone
2. Restart the Rugyeyo app
3. If many records are pending, sync them first

### Q: I recorded something by mistake
**A:**
- If not synced: You may be able to delete it
- If already synced: Contact your supervisor to correct it in the web system

## Tips for Best Use

1. **Sync regularly** - Don't let too many records pile up
2. **Take clear photos** - Make sure task completion photos are clear and show the work done
3. **Check your tasks daily** - Accept or reject tasks promptly
4. **Keep your phone charged** - Especially during field work
5. **Report problems early** - Tell your supervisor if something isn't working
